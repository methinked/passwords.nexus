Memorable passwords

Three words, one digit, one symbol — secure enough for everyday use. Generated locally in your browser, never sent anywhere.

Strength preset

    How it works

    Each password combines English words, a digit, and a symbol. The default Strong preset uses three words — like ForestRiverMist4!. Easier to recall than gibberish, stronger than password123.

    100% client-side

    Passwords are built with crypto.getRandomValues in your browser. Nothing is transmitted to any server. Open source and auditable on GitHub. Inspired by the original passwds.ninja.

    When to use what

    • Fair — 1 word + 1 digit + 1 symbol (forums, streaming)
    • Strong — 3 words + 1 digit + 1 symbol (recommended default)
    • Maximum — 3 words + 2 digits + 2 symbols (banking, work)
