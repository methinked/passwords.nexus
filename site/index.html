Memorable passwords

Three words, one digit, one symbol — secure enough for everyday use. Generated locally in your browser, never sent anywhere.

Strength preset

    How it works

    Each password combines familiar English words, a digit, and a symbol — like RiverStoneMist7! or Blue-Moon-Cloud3#. Easier to recall than random gibberish, stronger than password123.

    100% client-side

    Passwords are built with crypto.getRandomValues in your browser. Nothing is transmitted to any server. Inspired by the original passwds.ninja, reimagined for word-based passphrases.

    When to use what

    • Easy — everyday accounts (3 words + 1 digit + 1 symbol)
    • Standard — email, shopping, social media
    • Strong / Maximum — banking, work, password managers' master hints
